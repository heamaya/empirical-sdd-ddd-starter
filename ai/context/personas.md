# Personas

## Persona 1

### Name

Ana — The Aspiring Poet

### Goals

- Write poems regularly and keep them organized by theme or collection
- Get honest feedback on her work without being told what to write
- Understand what her writing style resembles so she can learn from poets she admires

### Pain Points

- Generic note apps have no poetry context — feedback would require pasting into ChatGPT manually
- AI writing tools try to autocomplete or generate — she wants to own her words
- Hard to know if her rhythm and imagery are working without a second opinion

### Main Workflows

1. Open or create a play for the current theme (e.g., "Winter Feelings")
2. Write a new poem with a title and body
3. Request an AI review to see grades and improvement directions
4. Optionally request a poet comparison to understand her stylistic influences

---

## Persona 2

### Name

Marco — The Curious Hobbyist

### Goals

- Write occasionally without pressure, keeping poems loosely organized
- Compare his style to famous poets out of curiosity
- Use the review feature selectively when he feels stuck

### Pain Points

- Doesn't want a complex app — just a clean space to write and occasionally reflect
- Doesn't care about saving drafts or versioning, just wants the current poem saved

### Main Workflows

1. Open the app, pick an existing play or create one quickly
2. Write or edit a poem
3. Trigger a poet comparison when curious about his style
