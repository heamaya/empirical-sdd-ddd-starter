# Project Vision

## Project Name

Versify — Poetry Writer Aider

---

## Problem Statement

Writers who want to develop their craft in poetry lack a focused, distraction-free space that helps them grow without prescribing what to write. Existing tools are either generic note-taking apps (no poetry awareness) or AI writing assistants that generate content for you (which undermines creative ownership).

Versify solves this by giving poets a structured writing space — organized by plays (folders) and poems — where AI acts as a thoughtful reviewer: grading, suggesting directions, and reflecting the writer's voice back at them through comparison with established poets.

---

## Users

- **Aspiring poets** — writers developing a poetry practice, seeking honest feedback without being told what to write
- **Hobbyist writers** — casual users who enjoy writing poems and want to reflect on their style
- **Students** — learners studying poetry who want to understand how their writing compares to known poets

---

## Core Features

- **Plays** — folders that group poems by theme, mood, or project
- **Poems** — individual pieces with a title and body, created and edited freely
- **AI Review** — on-demand analysis of a poem: grades it across defined aspects (rhythm, imagery, emotion, structure) and suggests improvement directions without writing for the user
- **Poet Comparison** — AI identifies which well-known poets the user's style resembles and explains why

---

## Constraints

- AI features are on-demand only — never automatic or intrusive
- AI must never generate poem content or complete sentences for the user
- Must remain lightweight: no paid infrastructure, single deployable unit
- Mobile-friendly UI (readable and writable on phone)
- No social features in scope — this is a personal tool

---

## Success Criteria

- A user can create a play, write a poem, and receive an AI review in under 2 minutes
- AI feedback feels like a coach, not a ghostwriter
- The app works end-to-end with a single deploy command
- Specs remain focused: 3–6 total, each completable in a day
